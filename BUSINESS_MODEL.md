## 4. Legal & Regulatory Compliance Safeguards

In accordance with the **Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021** and relevant guidelines:

1. **Non-Investment Disclaimer**: TRI Points/Coins are strictly non-monetary loyalty reward points. They are NOT cryptocurrency, securities, deposits, shares, or guaranteed investment returns.
2. **Anti-Pyramid Structure**: Rewards are strictly tied to verified ₹100 platform activations and product transactions, with anti-velocity limits preventing mass recursive payouts.
3. **Mandatory Disclosures**: 5 full compliance legal documents are embedded in the platform (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, Reward Policy, and Anti-Pyramid Disclosures).
4. **Cooling-Off Period**: Detailed policy guidelines for consumer dispute resolutions and cancellations.
